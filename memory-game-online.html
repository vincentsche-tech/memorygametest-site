How to play the memory game online

1) The board loads as a 4x4 grid of face-down cards. 2) Click any two cards to flip them. 3) If they match, they stay face up. 4) If not, they flip back — try to remember where they were. 5) Clear all 8 pairs with the fewest moves. Our online tracker records your moves and time so you can beat your own best.

Why play online instead of an app

An online memory game means zero storage used on your device, no permission prompts, and no "update available" nags. It is also shareable in one tap — send a friend the link and you're both playing the same classic game. For kids, students and seniors who just want a quick, familiar game, browser play removes every barrier.

Tips for a better online score

1) Memorize positions as you flip, even on misses. 2) Group cards by theme (fruit, shapes). 3) Slow down — accuracy beats speed. 4) Play a round daily; your visual memory strengthens with repetition.
